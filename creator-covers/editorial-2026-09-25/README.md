# Editorial covers for published creator works

Seven original AI-generated illustrations were prepared on 2026-09-25 for the eight public works listed in [manifest.json](manifest.json). The two *About me* entries share one cover because they have the same creator, title, and description. Each JPEG is 768 pixels wide and compressed for the catalog. These files are versioned in the web repository and served through Sakura's production web CDN from `/creator-covers/editorial-2026-09-25/`. The matching `creator_works.release_metadata.cover_url` rows hold their public URLs.

These are Sakura editorial illustrations for works that arrived without a cover. Creators can replace them with their own art through the usual upload flow. Existing creator-supplied covers were not changed.

## Generation prompts

All images used the built-in image generation tool in `illustration-story` mode, with portrait cover framing and no text, logo, or watermark.

- **Ledger:** Five broke art students in a night atelier, painted hands and brushes, a gilded empty frame, and the shadow of a dangerous collector; cinematic amber and ink-blue manga illustration.
- **About me:** An unspecified writer seen from behind at a sunlit journal, with abstract memories rising as petals and constellations; soft cream, blush, indigo, and gold. The image intentionally makes no claim about Milla's appearance.
- **Billie is hot:** A fictional stylish young adult woman in a neon-lit rainy city evening; contemporary, playful romance atmosphere. No likeness of a real Billie or celebrity.
- **Elusive sam:** A distant, anonymous figure in a misty violet alley with sakura petals and a warm streetlamp. The source has no meaningful synopsis, so the scene suggests mystery without claiming plot details.
- **Spongs:** An original rounded sea-sponge creature singing to a fish audience on a colorful underwater stage. No existing cartoon character design.
- **toly-san:** An original anime-style engineer on a rooftop, repairing red network fractures into teal and violet light above a futuristic city. No real-person likeness or crypto logo.
- **Time to Rescue Solana:** A fictional developer on a balcony at sunrise watching a networked city shift from red failures to violet and teal paths, with many builders at work.
